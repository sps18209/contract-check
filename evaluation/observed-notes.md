# Development observations, 2026-10-02

Four independent reviewer passes used the packaged Contract Check skill and only the fictional case text and instructions. The reviewers returned versioned projects and review records; no edits or client documents were involved. `observed-score.json` confirms record provenance and citation of each expected passage. These checks do not judge legal correctness or completeness.

| Case | Observed issue | Restraint and open questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Services bonus | Identified that `exceed $100,000` excludes equality, and proposed an inclusive threshold. | Preserved the 30-day invoice rule; raised measurement and year questions separately. |
| Software license | Identified that the Customer-only access restriction excludes negotiated majority-owned affiliates. | Kept unrelated sublicensing out and left the three-year confidentiality term in place. |
| Data processing | Identified post-appointment notice and the absence of an objection opportunity as separate mismatches. | Did not invent an absolute veto or statutory rule; treated missing Schedule A as unverified. |
| Property option | Identified calendar-versus-business days and dispatch-versus-receipt as separate mismatches. | Treated the holiday convention and effective exercise notice as open questions, not legal conclusions. |

These are short, prompted, fictional agreements. No revised clean copies were produced, so the preservation check after editing is `not tested`. The four cases cannot support a general accuracy claim. Next evaluation should include unseen long contracts with exhibits, tables, conflicting definitions, multiple reference styles, and lawyer-labeled expected findings, including negative examples. A lawyer must assess each proposed fix, not merely the cited passage.
