The draft and your instruction
Draft: “Vendor earns a $500 bonus when sales exceed $100,000.”

Your instruction: “We agreed the bonus starts at $100,000.”
A finding to review
Text: “exceed $100,000”
Issue: Exactly $100,000 does not satisfy this wording.
Effect: The threshold may differ from your stated deal.
Option: “equal or exceed $100,000.”
Decision: Confirm that the inclusive threshold was agreed.
Status: Proposal only; no edit applied.

Common questions

Know what you are downloading.

Does this website review my contract?

No. This site distributes Contract Check. You run the skill in your AI assistant. There is no contract-upload form or chatbot on this site.

Is SKILL.md alone the complete skill?

No. It is the instruction entry point. Download the complete ZIP or .skill archive to include the references, forms, Python engine, and manifest. Upload the archive through your host’s skill installation interface and enable it.

Does the ZIP contain everything?

Yes, all implemented instructions, references, forms, and Python runtime are included. The host supplies code execution, file access, research, PDF extraction, and exports where available.

Can it change terms without asking?

The workflow requires your decision on substantive proposals. The engine requires an approved plan tied to the current document and an adopted or custom finding for content changes. Check the results yourself; these controls do not prove the analysis is correct.

Is it ready to rely on for a legal decision?

It is a development release. It can help organize issues and proposed wording, but real-contract evaluation, lawyer review, and host-specific behavior checks remain separate requirements.

Do I need a connector?

No external connector is required for the bundled review workflow. A connector is useful only if you want an assistant to access an outside service. Check that service’s access and privacy settings before sharing documents.

Where does my contract go?

You provide it to your AI host, under that service’s settings and terms. This website does not receive it. Review confidentiality obligations before uploading a real agreement; a conversation with an AI does not itself establish privilege.
